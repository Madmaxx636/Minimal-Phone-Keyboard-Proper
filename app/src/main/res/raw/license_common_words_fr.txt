Common French words (common_words_fr.txt)

This list of words is derived from the "FrequencyWords" project (fr_full.txt, from the 2018 lists) by Hermit Dave:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords

That project's lists were made from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus, which is distributed by OPUS:
https://opus.nlpl.eu/

The content is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made to the list:
- It was cut down to the words that also appear in one of two lists of real French words (the "Words" and "French-Wordlist" GitHub projects), so that misspellings and foreign text picked up by the source's web crawl are left out.
- The counts were dropped. The words are only listed in order of how common they are.

This file, as changed, is available under the same license.
